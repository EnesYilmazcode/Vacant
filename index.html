Why it is different

Every app in this category computes “no class is scheduled, therefore free.” On a Saturday that reports over a thousand rooms free while most classroom buildings are locked. Vacant reads the opening hours Ohio State already publishes, which nobody else does.

It also answers the question that actually decides where you go: not when a room frees up, but how long it is yours once you finish walking to it, with the walk already subtracted.
